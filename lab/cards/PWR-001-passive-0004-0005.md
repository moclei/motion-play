| ID | Hypothesis | Source |

| M | Measurement artefact: lead off the pad or on the wrong pad, not settled (capacitive nets drift), lead resistance, wrong range | always listed |

**Magnitude (argued).** No passive difference was seen on +3.3V and +5V between the boards at V7R-020 (304.9 vs 304.4 Ω; same MΩ drift). A dead part on a power net usually shows as a < 100 Ω short or a diode reading off by > 100 mV; the ±10 % / ±30 mV match bands below sit well inside that and well outside DMM repeatability on a settled reading.

## Predictions and exclusions (written before the run)
"Match" for a point: settled ohms within ±10 % of #0005's, or both > 100 kΩ (still drifting at 15 s counts as > 100 kΩ if it's above that); diode within ±30 mV of #0005's, or both OL.
SW1 throw, read electrically: M (BOOST_EN) to G < 5 Ω → GND throw ("OFF"); M reads the same as L (VSYS_SENSE) within ±10 % → ON throw.
